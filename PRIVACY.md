# Privacy Policy

**CatSession Manager** is designed with privacy-by-design principles:

### 1. No Telemetry or Tracking
- The mod contains zero telemetry, zero analytics, and zero tracking code.
- No statistics, hardware identifiers, or usage logs are collected or reported.

### 2. Network Transmission
- The mod does not operate or communicate with any third-party backend servers, intermediate proxies, or webhooks.
- Network connections are made exclusively to official Microsoft and Mojang endpoints (`login.microsoftonline.com`, `user.auth.xboxlive.com`, `xsts.auth.xboxlive.com`, and `api.minecraftservices.com`) solely during interactive, user-initiated authentication flows.

### 3. Local Data Storage
- All session records remain on your local storage in the Minecraft configuration directory (`config/catsession/sessions.enc`).
- No data is synced to any cloud service.

### 4. Data Retention and Erasure
- Stored session data can be deleted at any time via the in-game GUI using **Delete Session** or **Clear All Data**.
